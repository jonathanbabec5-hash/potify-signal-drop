# Signal Drop 001 marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Event hosts and print collaborators. **Offer.** Connect a printed signal card to one verifiable ecosystem destination.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show a physical card scan and publish the decoded URL beside the artwork. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Scan the sample card. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 witnessed successful scans. |

### Sales test

Quote one event-specific print run only after scan acceptance and firm production cost. Track paid delivered batches independently of the number of scans. Seek one event organizer willing to order a small printed run after a proof scans on two phones.

### Measurement and decision

Use `signal-drop-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** A decoded QR proves the payload. Attendance, actual redirect destination and sales need separate receipts. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
