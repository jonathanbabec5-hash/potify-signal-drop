## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · QR component and decoding test**

### Goal and audience

Connect a printed signal card to one verifiable ecosystem destination. The immediate audience is event hosts and print collaborators.

### Design and implementation

The printed matrix from the Launch Room encodes the original campaign link. A regression test decodes the actual modules. Treat the encoded URL as an interface that survives a printed card. A readable design still fails if the quiet zone or module geometry is damaged.

### What a contributor can establish

The original QR regression test decoded the matrix to its fixed public URL. An actual printed scan on a physical device is outstanding. Check the actual print at its intended size on two phone cameras, then follow the destination to see where it lands today.

### Evidence and outstanding work

A decoded QR proves the payload. Attendance, actual redirect destination and sales need separate receipts. The next acceptance case is concrete. Render the matrix, decode it independently and compare its URL to the original approved destination.

### Place in POTIFY

Seek one event organizer willing to order a small printed run after a proof scans on two phones. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
